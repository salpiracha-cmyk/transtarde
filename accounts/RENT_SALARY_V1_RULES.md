# Transtrade V1 — Rent & Salary Rules

## Simple operating rule
Salary and rent are maintained in Accounts. Mill staff do not enter or maintain these masters.

## Salary Master
Salary records may be grouped as:
- Mill Staff
- Office Staff
- Home Staff
- Director Remuneration
- Home Monthly Give

Each record keeps the name, legal entity, category, net monthly salary/remuneration, monthly Zakat, other recurring allowances, start date, optional end date and notes. These are the only recurring Salary Master amounts in the first live version.

Accounts and the owner's Master Records screen edit the same Salary Master. Staff may be added, amended or removed from future Salary Sheets. Removing staff makes the record inactive and preserves prepared history.

When an amount changes, Accounts enters the same person/category with the new amount and a new start date. Transtrade closes the older rate on the previous day. Old months are not overwritten.

## Salary accounting treatment
- Mill Staff, Office Staff and Home Staff normally use Staff Cost.
- Home Monthly Give normally uses Family / Owner Allocation.
- Director Remuneration starts as To Confirm unless Accounts selects the approved treatment.
- A To Confirm item is not prepared into the monthly books until the treatment is selected.

The app title Director is an internal role/title. It does not by itself decide the legal or accounting treatment.

## Salary payments
Salary advances are not used in this version. Accounts prepares the month first, then records payments against that month's exact outstanding salary balance. Payment can only use an enabled PKR company bank account or company cash.

Net salary/remuneration, Zakat and other allowances remain separate in the Salary Sheet and journal detail. Zakat posts to the separate Zakat ledger.

## Monthly preparation
Prepare Month records what is due for the selected month. It does not mean that the amount has been paid.

If any salary item still needs an Accounts Treatment decision, Transtrade prepares the other valid items but clearly shows which item is still waiting.

## Donations
Donations appear under Expenses & Overheads and maintain three separate ledgers:
- Zakat
- Sadqa
- Fi Sabilillah

Salary-sheet Zakat and direct Zakat donations post to the Zakat ledger. Sadqa and Fi Sabilillah never mix into that ledger or into each other.

## Mill Rent Master
Each rent record keeps:
- Mill / location
- Landlord / payee
- Monthly rent amount
- Start date and optional end date
- Normal payment pattern
- Notes

Payment pattern may be Monthly, Quarterly, Half-Yearly, Twice-Yearly or Other.

When the rent changes, Accounts enters the same mill/payee with a new amount and start date. The previous rate is retained for earlier periods.

## Rent payments
Rent may be paid before or after the month is prepared.

- Payment against an already prepared month reduces the rent payable.
- Payment for a future month is kept as prepaid rent.
- When that future month is prepared, the prepaid amount is used automatically.
- One payment can cover several selected months.
- Transtrade blocks payment above the remaining rent for the selected months.

## Production cost
Only these fixed monthly costs may feed the Pakistan rice production-cost calculation:
- Mill Staff salary marked for production cost
- Mill Rent marked for production cost

Office Staff, Home Staff, Home Monthly Give and Director Remuneration do not automatically increase rice production cost.

For a month with eligible fixed mill costs, Transtrade spreads the prepared Mill Staff salary + Mill Rent over the actual Ready Rice quantity produced in that month. Accounts sees the fixed cost per kg before final production-cost approval.

If an eligible Mill Staff salary or Mill Rent month has not yet been prepared, the related production stock cost stays Waiting.

## Mill screen rule
This workflow must not add fields or change the Mill salary/rent screens. Any future change that affects what Mill users see or do requires owner approval first.
