# Director / family monthly payments

Director and Mrs monthly entitlement appears first, with eligible advance recovery and the monthly amount after recovery. Opening Pay prepares the remaining monthly payment. Each actual payment has one date and funding account. Its total is calculated from the monthly allocation and additional rows. The + adds an Expense or Capital row with amount and narration; − removes that unsaved row. Another actual payment supports a different date/account. Existing split-beneficiary and routed investment payments remain editable.

Expense choices resolve active company subledgers and their current heads. Vehicle costs require a registered vehicle; vehicle tax also requires its period. Production costs retain their dedicated workflow. Capital choices use existing properties/assets, direct broker funding, or the existing optional routing workflow. Property payments update the asset's instalment history and balance; broker funding creates the canonical investment event. Extra payments do not increase or discharge monthly salary entitlement. Final completion credits bank/cash once per actual transaction. Draft saves have no monetary effect. Whole-payment reversal restores entitlement, investment balances and active asset instalments, retaining the original in correction history.

The salary sheet now has five columns. Group is beneath the name, amount components remain in Breakdown, and Pay/Amend, Adjust month, recurring edit and remove stay beside the person. The salary popup can use 1480px/98vw; narrow screens use stacked rows.
