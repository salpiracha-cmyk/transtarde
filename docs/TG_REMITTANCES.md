# TG remittance review

A Pakistan credit advice received from TG posts the TTI/BRM receipt immediately. Its USD/AED principal creates a pending TG remittance under the same Accounts lock. The principal reduces the selected TG bank's available balance, without creating a TG journal until Accounts confirms it.

TG Held / Incomplete Entries and Review TG Remittances open the confirmation window. Select the credit advices belonging to one actual remittance; combining requires the same bank, currency and receiving company. Confirm the bank date, optional online reference, bank charges and actual VAT amount. Charges/VAT can use a different TG bank and its currency. POST produces one balanced AED accounting journal with native bank-currency amounts, releases the pending reserve, and links every Pakistan receipt. It does not block an overdraft. Repeated POST requests cannot deduct twice.

Banks/Cash and the TG dashboard show available balances with posted and pending amounts. USD bank ledgers retain native USD values; other TG ledgers offer USD transactions or AED books. Foreign-exchange differences remain in AED. Missing historic native figures are marked unavailable rather than guessed.

Older automatically mirrored receipts are already deducted. They appear for review with no new reservation. Confirmation with a reason reverses the old mirrors and consolidates their principal once, adding the charges/VAT. Original journals and reversals remain auditable. No production transaction is silently migrated on page load. Edit on the confirmed TG Post ID reopens the complete remittance with a reason, reversing it and returning the principal to pending review. Pakistan receipts can then be corrected through their normal amendment workflow before reconfirming TG.

Invoice allocations include their invoice references. Advance narrations follow an exact, unambiguous FI/credit-advice match and committed FI utilisation against the exact lot. Both FI and Customs allocation records must agree, and Customs must be saved with the correct company/currency. Partial utilisation retains the amount per invoice. Saved TG Pack Customs invoice references take precedence for TG shipments. Export commits persist this metadata; ledger/search reads also project it from the current committed records. Narration updates never create payments or alter financial amounts, and removing an allocation removes its generated reference.
