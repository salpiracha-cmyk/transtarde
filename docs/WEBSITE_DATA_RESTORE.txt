TRANSTRADE — RESTORE WEBSITE DATA

This restores saved business data to a running Transtrade website. Opening an
offline Recovery folder alone does not change the website.

WHAT YOU NEED
1. The password-protected TRANSTRADE_DATA ZIP, or an older TRANSTRADE_FULL ZIP.
2. The ZIP password that was used when that particular file was created.
3. Your current Super Admin login.
4. Working internet, an awake computer and an available backup drive.
5. A running, compatible Transtrade installation with enough free hosting space
   for the upload, its unpacked data and a safety copy of current records.

Do not choose a Software-only ZIP or a readable CSV export for this procedure.
The complete ZIP must be smaller than 4 GB. It is uploaded in 1 MB pieces; you
do not split or unpack it yourself. Expanded contents are limited to 16 GiB.

RESTORE ON THE EXISTING WEBSITE
1. Ask all staff to stop entering or changing records. Make a fresh backup first
   if the current website still contains records you may need.
2. Sign in as Super Admin.
3. Open Backup & Recovery → Open Restore / Continue recovery.
   Direct address: https://app.transtradeinternational.com/restore.php
4. Choose the Data or older Complete ZIP from your PC or pen drive.
5. Enter that ZIP's backup password. This is not your website login password
   or emergency admin recovery code.
6. Click Upload & verify / Resume upload. Keep the page open.
   The page identifies the file, uploads pieces and verifies the encrypted data.
   Uploading and verification do not replace live business records.
7. Review the displayed filename, backup date, file count and software revisions.
   The snapshot affects Accounts, Mill, Exports, Directors, master records,
   settings and uploaded documents. Later entries will not remain in live books.
8. When you are certain this is the right recovery point, type RESTORE and click
   Confirm Restore. Accept the final confirmation message.
9. Transtrade pauses other requests, creates and checks a safety copy, replaces
   the saved data, restores a configured supported MySQL database when present,
   and checks the result. Do not restart setup or change hosting files during it.
10. Wait for RESTORE COMPLETE. Current user accounts, login passwords, roles,
    backup password settings and PC connection remain in place. Existing
    sessions are ended so an old open form cannot save over recovered records.
11. Sign in again. Check the backup-date totals and several known records:
    - Accounts: trial balance, ledgers, bank/petty cash and recent postings.
    - Mill: stocks, arrivals, production, recoveries and bags.
    - Exports: contracts, shipments, lots and original documents.
    - Directors: reports and saved records.
12. Allow staff to resume only after your checks. Make a new verified backup.

IF INTERNET OR POWER STOPS
- During upload: reopen Restore, select exactly the same ZIP, enter its password
  again, and click Upload & verify / Resume upload. Completed pieces are reused.
- During verification: use the same file and password; verified progress remains.
- After Confirm Restore: reopen Restore and click Continue recovery. You do not
  need to upload the ZIP or enter its password again for the confirmed operation.
  The site stays paused until the restore or its rollback finishes.
- A lost final response does not run the restore twice. Sign in again and open
  Restore to read its recorded result.
- If the page says it is reverting, continue until it confirms the previous data
  was recovered. If it repeatedly cannot continue, leave the site paused, keep
  the original ZIP and have support resolve the storage/server error. Do not
  delete the private recovery workspace or maintenance marker yourself.

CLEAR PREPARED UPLOAD
This removes the hosted upload and its temporary unpacked copy only when no
restore is active. It does not remove your PC/pen-drive original or the retained
pre-restore safety copy. Clear a finished upload before choosing another backup.
At most seven website-restore safety copies are retained by this flow; another
restore is held for administrator review rather than deleting safety copies.
These are pre-restore protection, not a routine hosted backup schedule.

MOVING TO HOSTINGER OR ANOTHER HOST
1. Upload the matching Full Software For Upload On Hosting package to the new
   site's public_html, including hidden hosting configuration files.
2. Keep transtrade_private outside public_html. Never upload private data to a
   publicly accessible folder or GitHub.
3. Configure HTTPS, PHP and any database required by that installation. Complete
   the initial owner setup so you can sign in as Super Admin.
4. Open Backup & Recovery on the new website and follow the restore steps above.
   The restore uploads DATA to that hosting; it does not install application code.
5. A MySQL installation needs the corresponding complete database snapshot and
   compatible InnoDB tables. Incompatible configurations are refused before live
   data replacement. This flow does not automatically provision a database or
   convert a file-only backup into MySQL.
6. Check all four modules before moving the domain or allowing staff to work.

PASSWORDS AND IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS
- ZIP password: opens that encrypted archive. Old ZIPs keep their old password.
- Super Admin login: authorizes access and the final restore confirmation.
- Emergency recovery code: recovers Super Admin access; it cannot open a ZIP.
- Offline Recovery: produces readable folders and hosting files on your PC.
- Website Restore: replaces the website's saved data after Confirm Restore.
- Software upload: installs or updates code separately from website data restore.

Keep the original backup and its password separately. Never delete your last
verified backup while testing a replacement.
