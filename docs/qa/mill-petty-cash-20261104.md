# Mill Petty Cash — 4 November 2026

Owner requested an audit of entries, receipt/payment balance, typing and optional auto-save for narration/expense type. No production transactions are to be created by QA.

Confirmed gap: the existing Milling expense form committed tt33pettyexp operational history, while its large balance read only posted Accounts journals. Saving a Mill expense therefore did not reduce the displayed confirmed custody balance. Historic operational rows have no verified financial linkage and must not automatically be reposted or silently subtracted a second time.

New explicitly reviewed Mill payments reuse dex_post and the original generalExpenses/journal engine. The existing mill_cash_ledger endpoint now supports a narrow authenticated payment action with Mill Petty Cash View/Create, business company grants, physical owned mill, CSRF, Accounts revision, stable request fingerprint and retry guards. The mill cash payment source is its original 1120 custody identity. Dated/future-spending validation prevents overdrafts; future cash payments are rejected. New payments are visible once in both Accounts and Milling, and corrections use their original Accounts expense record.

The read model uses pc_rows, the canonical cash/reversal/amendment projection. Future rows and reversed/amended originals no longer inflate the current Mill balance. Receipt/payment totals, latest-first entries, chronological running balances, Post ID, payee, expense purpose and narration are shown. The existing historical ledger/date-range controls and separate operational history remain available. Failed reads show unavailable rather than zero; explicit refresh preserves the active draft and rejects late responses after mill changes.

Per the owner's current request, an optional local text-memory tick saves only narration/expense type on this device, scoped to user/company/mill/category. It never commits a financial transaction, starts a timed refresh, or restores an old amount as a new payment. Previously posted text supplies suggestions. An exact pending payment snapshot is durably retained separately for explicit confirmation after an uncertain response. Deterministic stale-revision errors restore editing; network/ambiguous acknowledgements retain the original retry identity. Cancellation retains the entry. No historic operational records are automatically migrated into journals.

Focused disposable tests: staff payment -> canonical expense and Super Admin ledger, cents/arithmetic, categories, zero-write review, missing fields, invalid amounts, insufficient/date/future funds, reversed/future exclusion, exact retries and changed-payload conflicts, company/location/CSRF/permission boundaries, Accounts amendment, text drafts and suggestions, typing-only preview, refresh preservation, cancellation, uncertain response recovery and stale edits. Existing transfer/projection/authorization/asset-delivery tests are retained. Full Accounts and Milling/Exports CI plus zero-write live Mill/Accounts reconciliation are required for release.

Production incident evidence, final release commit, test run IDs and actual live balance/counts will be recorded in the release PR after verification. No live test trucks, expenses or cash transfers are authorized by this QA.
