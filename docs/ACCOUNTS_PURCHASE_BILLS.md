# Accounts purchase bill rules

Broker and supplier are independent searchable filters over the approved Soda relationships. Selecting a supplier selects its broker only when that relationship is unique. Filtering by broker does not change the commodity payee or hide broker-only Sodas. The approved Soda remains authoritative: supplier receives commodity liability when present; otherwise broker receives it. Brokerage and broker WHT remain separate ledger components.

For Ready Rice, the combine option is beside the truck/container selector and labelled **COMBINE THE BILLS IN THIS SODA**. It defaults off. Enabling it initially selects all eligible unbilled trucks in that Soda. Each receipt keeps its own due date. Cash is receipt date plus two days; credit is receipt date plus approved credit days. The summary shows the date range when selected receipts have different due dates.

Ready Rice net quantity is weighbridge weight less total bags multiplied by empty-bag weight. Bag weight is entered in grams and converted to kg. Bag count and tare are populated from exact linked Milling records where available and remain editable for bill verification. The calculation does not edit Milling or Export weights. Commodity value and brokery both use net rice quantity. Rice brokery is net kg / 100 multiplied by rate. For Raw Rice, the existing accepted Pohanch quantity remains the basis and one bill uses one Pohanch.

Ready Rice calculation order: gross commodity value; empty-bag deduction; brokerage; Kanta (selected truck/container count multiplied by rate); loading charges (saved Loading/Milling bag count multiplied by user-entered rate per bag); additional additions/deductions; bill amount; broker WHT (default 15%); final bill payable. There are no filling charges on Ready Rice. The same editable brokery rate and per-100-kg basis are available on Raw Rice. A successfully posted rice bill saves a positive buying-brokery rate to the existing broker master effective on the bill date; selling rates and previously posted bills remain unchanged.

The server calculates the bag deduction, Kanta, loading charges, brokery and WHT again, validates the commodity total, prevents duplicate source billing and preserves supplier/broker allocations. Bill posting and subsequent hold synchronization send the selected legal entity. A refresh failure after posting must not report a failed posting or encourage a duplicate. All QA runs use disposable storage and are removed when the tests finish.

Loading charges have a permanent row immediately below Kanta. Loading bag count is read-only and summed over selected saved receipts; editing the empty-bag verification count does not alter it. The server uses the saved receipt bag count, ignores a submitted loading count and retains quantity, rate and amount with the posted bill. Additional rows show their delete control first; all amounts are bold and aligned at the right.

Local Sales payments, Local Sales costing and Export costing requests carry the selected company, including the COGS helper and workflow requests. Cost posting is limited to that company; payment review rejects records belonging to another company. Bill mounting coalesces concurrent opens and does not replace an existing draft on delayed background mounts. Full-screen QA exercises an ordinary Accounts user, company selection, bill entry, loading totals, delayed mounts and company-context error responses.
